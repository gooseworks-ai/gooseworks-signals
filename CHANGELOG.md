# Changelog

## 3.5.1

- The plugin lists its support, privacy and terms pages for app directories.
- Saving brand facts is now called brain_save, since the change saves at once.
- The app is offered in 157 countries, leaving out 13 territories ChatGPT can't save in review details.

## 3.5.0

- Goose finds gaps in your Klaviyo, like a live flow with one email, a flow left in draft, a list that starts no flow, a campaign with no audience or a template with an unfinished link, and fixes the ones you pick. It never sends or schedules a campaign.
- Goose reads your Pinterest campaigns, ad groups and ads with their results, and makes or changes a campaign when you ask. A campaign it makes starts paused.

## 3.4.0

- Each change Goose makes is its own action, so your AI app can ask before the ones that remove or overwrite something: adding or learning a brand, learning from what you share, uploading and saving a file, writing a document, sharing a link, downloading a folder, researching a competitor, and saving, removing or watching inspiration.
- Uploads work one way everywhere: a logo, font, product photo or any other file goes in with the same two steps.
- Blog feeds are added with the other sources Goose reads.

## 3.3.1

- GooseWorks now lives at growth.gooseworks.ai, and the plugin connects to mcp.growth.gooseworks.ai.

## 3.3.0

- The plugin is now called GooseWorks Signals.
- Pages show in the chat as a card, with a button that opens them in Goose.
- Pages can start from a template, like a report, a plan, a competitor teardown or an ads health check, and read the brand's Meta, Google Analytics, Google Ads, facts, competitors and files straight from Goose, live or as a snapshot.
- New blocks: lists, sheets that open from a row, switches, columns, folded sections, diagrams, video with captions, embeds, files, steps, meters, boards, pivots, checklists, timelines, galleries, an action button, and html for anything else.
- Goose brings in images, videos and captions from a public link, and checks how a page draws on a phone and a desktop.
- Goose shares a page with an as-of-now link, turns links off, and reads and adds comments.

## 3.2.0

- Goose can make pages for a brand: reports, teardowns and plans with summaries, numbers, tables, charts and images. They stay private to the brand's organization, under Pages in Files, and keep every version.

## 3.1.0

- Reading and changing are now separate tools. Fresh Meta numbers come from meta_refresh, and brand sources split into sources_read and sources_change. A brand's share links and folder downloads are read from file_list.
- Every tool picks what it does with view when it only reads, and with step when it changes something.
- The meta-ads skill is now ads-and-site-results, since it also reads website analytics. It says when its work is done.
- Brand setup and brand files say which one handles a file Goose should learn from.
- Tool descriptions say when to use a nearby tool instead, and brand reading and search fields describe themselves.

## 3.0.1

- Brand setup reads LinkedIn company posts and finds software, services and plans on the brand's website.
- Suggested competitors stay suggested until the person picks Watch. Watching one that has a website starts reading it straight away.
- New tools list and read a competitor's actual ads, and save one the person picks.

## 3.0.0

This V3 package keeps brand setup and knowledge, connected business data, competitor and social research, supplied footage search, general files, and paid-research approval and progress. Plugin packaging and browser sign-in remain available for supported AI apps.
