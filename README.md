# GooseWorks Signals

GooseWorks keeps your brand knowledge, reads connected business data, researches competitors and social posts, and manages your brand's files inside your AI app.

## What you can ask

- "Set up my brand from our website."
- "What do you know about us?"
- "What are my competitors running?"
- "Find Instagram posts about loose-leaf tea."
- "How are my ads doing?"
- "Where did our website visits come from last week?"
- "Save this research as a document."
- "Share this folder with a link."

## How it works

Goose reads your website and the sources you supply. Goose saves what it learns on My brand, where you can edit it. Connect business accounts on Integrations to read their data. You make ad and budget changes in Ads Manager.

Paid research shows its price in credits first. Approve it on its card or at its link in Goose. A yes in chat cannot spend credits. The job card follows the research and lets you stop it.

## What's inside

The six skills cover brand setup, brand files, competitor research, social inspiration, supplied footage search, and ad and website results. The connector files keep the GooseWorks address and browser sign-in for Claude, ChatGPT, Codex and Cursor.
